About these seven verses

This deck contains the seven verses of Durgā Saptaślokī, selected from Durgā Saptaśatī / Devī Māhātmyam. It does not contain all 700 recitation units or the introductory invocation.

  1. Sanskrit Documents: Durgā Saptaślokī
    Base Sanskrit text. Verse numbering 1–7.
  2. Sanskrit Documents: full Devī Māhātmyam
    Locations: 1.55–56, 4.17, 11.10, 11.12, 11.24, 11.29, 11.39. Verse numbering can differ between editions.
  3. Learn Sanskrit: vowel length and sandhi
    Basis for the pronunciation and joined-form notes.

English glosses and conversational Telugu explanations are prepared for this learning deck. The explanation gives the meaning of the whole verse in simple language. Spelling variants are distinguished from meaning changes. The devotional claims are presented as the meaning of the hymn.

The supplied Sanskrit Channel image inspired the teaching layout. It depicts Lalita Sahasranama, a different text. No audio from that channel is used.
