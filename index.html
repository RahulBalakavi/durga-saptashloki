The full Saptashati

All 700 traditionally numbered units across 13 chapters are included. This edition groups 22 repeated refrains into ranges, giving 656 complete passages. Speaker announcements are included in the traditional numbering. No passage is duplicated merely to make the slide count reach 700.

Sanskrit source: Sanskrit Documents

Chapter counts: 104, 69, 44, 42, 129, 24, 27, 63, 41, 32, 55, 41, 29. Preliminary Kavacha, Argala, Kilaka and other supplementary prayers are outside these 700 units.

The English and everyday Telugu translations are AI-assisted learning translations drafted and checked against the Sanskrit. They explain complete passages, without word-by-word commentary. They are not a critical scholarly edition.

Historical reference consulted: F. E. Pargiter’s public-domain 1904 translation of the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, chapters 81–93. Read the historical translation. The note on 7.20 also consults the Śabdakalpadruma dictionary entry for mahāsiṃha.
